Coherence rules can be under two forms:
two mention rules: 
Has four arguments
the relation r1, (from any mention m1 to any mention m2)
the relation r2, that is the consequence 
the negation (if r1 means r2 there is no negation, but if m1 means no r2 then there is a negation)
the orientation of consequence (if r2 (or no r2) is from m1 to m2 or from m2 to m1)
three mentions rules:
Same thing except there are two observed relations:
r1 (between m1 and m2) and r2 (between m2 and m3)
and one consequence relation r3 (between m1 and m3 (or m3 and m1 depending on the direction of course))
